## D. Arm B / sensitivity and hazard-estimand claims

**D1. Tie-breaking and the fitting-distribution headline. — Mechanism and numbers VERIFIED; the review's implied non-disclosure is NOT SUPPORTED for the body text, but its charge stands against the abstract and the reported shares.**
- The project's own diagnosis (`cdpe_sensitivity/DISTRIBUTION_DIAGNOSIS.md`, 2026-10-05) establishes the mechanism: the empirical (Gringorten) transform caps |z| at 2.146 for n=35, leaving ~128 land cells tied at each pole value every month; the detector's first-occurrence argmin/argmax resolves ties to the southernmost/westernmost cell (median empirical dry-pole latitude 27.5°N vs 30.5°N log-logistic), where the 4° buffer usually fails the ≤10% missing-data gate. Re-running with pole ties broken uniformly at random gives **57–80 CDPE months** (5 seeds, mean 72.0) at the baseline configuration instead of **6** (log-logistic: 44). The diagnosis concludes the masked Arm B distribution share (0.188) and distribution×threshold interaction (0.167) "are contaminated by this artifact."
- **Both manuscripts disclose this in the results text** (line 84, both files): *"Part of the distribution effect is a detector artifact rather than a property of either standardization: the empirical transform ties many cells at each pole value, the detector's first-occurrence rule parks the poles at the southernmost tied cell, and the coastal missing-data gate then rejects most of those months. Breaking pole ties at random returns 57 to 80 CDPE months at the Arm B baseline configuration rather than 6 under the empirical fit (44 under the log-logistic fit)."*
- What remains true in the review's charge: (a) the **abstract** still says "the detection threshold and the index fitting distribution dominate CDPE frequency" with no artifact caveat; (b) the **reported variance shares** (Arm B: distribution 0.19 + interaction 0.17) are computed from the unfixed first-occurrence rule — the diagnosis's own options ((a) retain + caveat, (b) declared tie rule, (c) continuous empirical variant) were resolved in favour of (a), so the shares stand as detector-dependent quantities. Fixing the tie rule and re-reporting corrected shares is a real, still-open item (fix list, class ii).
